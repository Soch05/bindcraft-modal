Six de novo miniproteins (57-94 aa) against EGFR domain III, ranked by predicted pH
selectivity first, mouse cross-reactivity second, interface quality third. Generated with
BindCraft 2.0, then evaluated by three models that took no part in generating them.

THE RESULT THIS SUBMISSION RESTS ON. Rank 1 raises the pKa of target histidine H409 by +2.84
units on its own AlphaFold2 design model, +2.27 on an independently predicted Boltz-2
structure, and +2.41 on the complex with the mouse target. A positive shift means binding
stabilises the protonated imidazole, so binding is thermodynamically favoured at pH 6.5 over
pH 7.4. PROPKA attributes the shift to two binder carboxylates, ASP56 and GLU73, each engaging
a different imidazole nitrogen; the desolvation term is negative, so this is not a burial
artifact. It is the only design in the set positive on all three measurements.

THE CAVEAT, NOT SOFTENED. Of 23 candidates examined, two showed a positive shift on their
design model and one survived verification on a second structure and a second species. Ranks
2 and 3 carry a mechanism that does not reproduce across predictors; ranks 4 to 6 carry none.
Seven further candidates were predicted counter-selective and were not submitted. The reason
for the low hit rate is structural rather than bad luck: no structure-based generator sees
protonation states, so this set was generated against affinity and pH was quantified
afterwards.

METHOD. pH conditionality is scored as a proton-ligand linkage: PROPKA 3 on the complex, the
target alone and the binder alone, all extracted from the same file, giving the pKa shift of
H409 on binding and a predicted ratio of affinity between the two assay pH values. Residue
numbering was verified on all 23 structures by recovering the full six-histidine fingerprint
of the target. An unbiased variant of the score, taken over every ionisable group rather than
H409 alone, agrees on the leading designs and never rescues a candidate.

Poses were re-scored with Boltz-2, independent of AlphaFold2 in architecture and weights, with
a precomputed deep MSA for the target and an empty MSA for the de novo binder. Agreement is
measured as contact recovery rather than RMSD, because RMSD forgives a rotation that preserves
the patch while losing every residue pairing. All 23 designs recover between 0.70 and 0.97.
Both models were trained on the PDB, so their agreement excludes an artifact specific to one
of them, not a bias shared by both.

Mouse cross-reactivity was measured rather than approximated. The Q01279 domain III sequence
was derived by two independent routes that agree, 87.4 percent identity with no indels and
H409 conserved. Binding confidence barely changes between species, mean delta iptm -0.016, but
three designs lose roughly half their epitope on the mouse target and a sequence-identity
proxy does not separate them.

A design rule was extracted and then tested against itself: a single salt bridge to H409, even
with ideal geometry, does not shift the pKa, whereas two distinct carboxylates each reaching a
different imidazole nitrogen do. The rule separates cleanly within one predictor, with a 2.20
angstrom margin, and fails when geometry from one predictor is combined with pKa from another,
which bounds its use. It supports three positive cases against three negatives and is reported
as suggestive rather than established. It enters no ranking criterion.

RANK 6 IS A NEGATIVE CONTROL, not a sixth candidate. It is expected to bind human EGFR and not
mouse: it recovers only 0.479 of its human epitope against the mouse target, the lowest in the
set, with the lowest ipSAE in the target-to-binder direction and a mouse pKa shift of -2.62. It
is the only submitted design carrying a directional, falsifiable prediction. If it binds human
and not mouse, the epitope analysis is validated; if it binds mouse anyway, the structural
cross-reactivity measurement used here needs recalibration. Both outcomes are informative.

ATTACHED. METHODS.md is the full methods paper, including the limitations and two mistakes
made during the work. DESIGN_METRICS.md holds per-design metrics as readable tables, with the
sequences and metrics of the 17 candidates that were considered and not submitted, so the
funnel can be checked. egfr_metadata.zip holds the machine-readable metrics with a dictionary
giving the provenance and calibration status of every column, the full provenance of models
and settings, and the predicted structures of the six submitted designs from the design model
and from the folding model against both the human and the mouse target.

Metrics reported per design: ipTM and ipAE from the design model, ipSAE and LIS from the
reference implementation, contact recovery against the independent folding model, ProteinMPNN
sequence recovery, pKa shifts on three structures, mouse epitope recovery, buried area,
hotspot and off-epitope contact fractions, solvent-exposure-weighted sequence liabilities, net
charge, and ESM-2 pseudo log-likelihood. No design carries a free cysteine. ESM-2 is reported
and excluded from every ordering, since it measures resemblance to natural proteins while the
competition requires novelty.
