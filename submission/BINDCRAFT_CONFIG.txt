  Hotspot rationale: 4/4 identical in mouse, 16.73 A CA span. A323 is an exposed acidic

  designed around. A359 was set as a coldspot because it is a human histidine that becomes
  arginine in mouse, which would break cross-reactivity.

NOT REPRODUCIBLE AT THE SEQUENCE LEVEL
  Replaying the same commit and settings does not return the same designs. JAX GPU
  reductions are not bit-deterministic and a gradient trajectory is chaotic: backbones
  accepted in one campaign died at an earlier stage in another at identical seed and
  settings digest. The method is reproducible, the sequences are not.

DOWNSTREAM, not part of BindCraft
  Everything used to rank these designs sits downstream of generation and took no part in
  it: PROPKA 3 for pKa shifts, Boltz-2 2.2.0 as an independent folding model, ProteinMPNN
  v_48_020 for sequence self-consistency, and ESM-2 as a descriptive column excluded from
  every ordering.
